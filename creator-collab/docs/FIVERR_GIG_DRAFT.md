## FAQ

**Which tools and platforms can you connect?**

It depends on the package and on available official APIs or supported
connectors. Share your current tools in the requirements and I will confirm the
feasible scope before implementation.

**Do you need my passwords?**

No passwords should be sent in order requirements. Where access is necessary,
use secure invitations, OAuth or platform-specific credentials with minimum
permissions.

**Are paid apps or API fees included?**

No. Any paid third-party service must be disclosed and approved separately
before it is used.

**Can you automate social media?**

Yes, where the platform officially supports the requested workflow. I do not
offer fake engagement, bulk following, spam or policy circumvention.

**Is a dashboard included?**

BASIC has no full dashboard. STANDARD may include light reporting or a small
dashboard if the agreed scope fits. PREMIUM includes one dashboard.

**What counts as a revision?**

A revision adjusts the agreed workflow. New integrations, workflows or major
features are a scope change and require a custom offer.

**Do I receive documentation?**

Yes. Every package includes an appropriate handover note; STANDARD and PREMIUM
include fuller documentation for the delivered system.

**Is ongoing maintenance included?**

PREMIUM includes up to seven calendar days of post-delivery support. Longer
maintenance is available only through a separate custom offer.

**Can you guarantee time savings, reach or revenue?**

No. I deliver the agreed workflow and verification evidence, but business or
platform performance cannot be guaranteed.
