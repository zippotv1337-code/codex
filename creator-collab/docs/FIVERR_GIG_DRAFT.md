# Fiverr Gig 1 — AI Workflow Automation

## Title

I will build AI workflow automation for your business

## Description

> I will design and build a practical AI workflow automation for your business.
> The goal is simple: reduce repetitive work, connect the tools you already use,
> and leave you with a documented workflow that your team can operate.
>
> We start with your real use case. I map the steps, define the inputs and
> outputs, build the agreed automation, test the happy path, and hand over a
> concise setup guide. Typical projects include lead routing, content
> operations, internal notifications, spreadsheet-to-dashboard flows, and
> customer-intake automation.
>
> Please message before ordering if your request involves regulated data,
> custom paid services, or an integration not listed in the package scope.
> I do not purchase subscriptions, invent credentials, or access private
> accounts on your behalf.

## FAQ

1. **Which tools can you connect?** Common business tools, subject to the
   agreed package scope and the access you provide.
2. **Do you buy subscriptions or API credits?** No. Paid services remain the
   buyer's decision and cost.
3. **Do I need to provide access?** Yes, only through the tool's normal
   permission path and only after we agree the scope.
4. **Can you automate every process?** No. A short pre-order check prevents
   unsuitable or unsafe automation requests.
5. **Do you work with sensitive data?** Only after a clear scope review; do
   not send passwords, OTPs, or personal identity documents in Fiverr chat.
6. **What is a revision?** A revision adjusts the agreed workflow; it is not a
   new unrelated workflow.
7. **Will I receive documentation?** Yes, every package includes a concise
   handover note.
8. **Can you provide ongoing support?** Premium includes seven calendar days
   of post-delivery support for the delivered scope.
9. **Can you build a dashboard?** The Premium package includes one agreed
   lightweight dashboard.
